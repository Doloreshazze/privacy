Privacy Policy for Applications by Playeverywhere

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Welcome to applications developed by Playeverywhere (“we”, “us”, “our”, or “Developer”).
We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency and data protection in accordance with applicable laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This Privacy Policy explains what data may be processed in our applications and how it is handled.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published under the Playeverywhere developer account on Google Play or other platforms (“Applications”).

Some Applications may contain advertising, while others may function fully offline. Data processing may differ depending on the specific Application. An app-specific privacy policy takes precedence over this general policy for that application.

2. Data We Collect

A. Data Collected by the Application Itself

Our Applications:

- Do not require account registration  
- Do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers  
- Do not collect precise location data  
- Do not access personal files or contacts  

We may process limited technical information necessary for functionality, such as:

- Device type  
- Operating system version  
- App version  
- Basic crash diagnostics (if provided automatically by the platform)  

We do not use this information to identify users by name. Technical identifiers and event data can nevertheless be personal or pseudonymous data rather than anonymous information.

B. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some of our Applications may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Whether advertising is present, and whether it uses test or production ads, is described in the policy for the specific Application. Do not treat a test advertisement as a guarantee of no data processing.

If advertising is enabled in a specific Application, AdMob may automatically collect certain information to provide ads, including:

- Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)  
- IP address (for general location estimation)  
- Device information  
- Ad interaction data  

Google receives and processes SDK data. Playeverywhere remains responsible for its own choices about integrating and configuring these services.

For more information, please refer to:
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Analytics, Advertising Choices & Consent

Some Applications may use Google Analytics for Firebase. When enabled, it can process game or app events, pseudonymous app-instance identifiers, device and app details, sessions and approximate IP-derived location. The app-specific policy explains whether it is currently enabled, its purposes, retention and available choices.

For Groove Parade, cloud analytics requires both a configured Firebase project and the player's separate opt-in in Settings. The switch is off by default. Withdrawing that choice stops further cloud analytics; it does not itself delete previously transmitted data. The current project is not connected. Google demo ad requests are separate from Firebase analytics consent.

Where consent is required, the relevant in-app controls must be provided before the applicable processing. Declining personalization does not itself authorize all other ad processing, and device advertising-ID controls do not replace an app's consent controls. Do not assume that every Application already has a production advertising consent form; consult its app-specific policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

Some Applications (such as games) may be suitable for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

For Applications that include advertising and are directed to children:

- Personalized advertising is disabled  
- Only non-personalized ads (if any) may be shown  
- No behavioral profiling is performed by us  

If you believe a child has provided personal data directly to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Parents are encouraged to supervise children’s app usage.

5. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

We do not create user profiles.

We do not track users across applications for our own purposes.

6. Data Security

7. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers.

Third-party services (such as advertising providers, if used) may retain data according to their own privacy policies.
