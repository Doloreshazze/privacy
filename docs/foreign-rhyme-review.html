Foreign Rhyme background playback

Android 15 demonstration of Foreign Rhyme 0.3.1 (com.playeverywhere.foreignrhyme).

In the bakery lesson, the learner turns on repeat and presses Play. After going to the home screen, the Android media controls still show the playing lesson and offer Pause and Stop.

The app starts playback only after the learner presses Play. A delay or an interruption would break the rhythm used to practice the verse. The media notification lets the learner pause or stop playback while using another app.
